Polygala

Butterflybushes, Milkwort, Snakeroot

  • Both images in the folder right now are of the species Polygala Myrtifolia, a.k.a. Mytle-leaf Milkwort
  • It used to be believed that cows that grazed on this plant would produce more milk
  • Native to much of the world in temperate and topical zones
